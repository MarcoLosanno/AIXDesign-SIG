# Identifying Implicit Assumptions About AI in Engineering Design

This repository contains the search queries and selected scientific papers used in the study of implicit assumptions underlying Artificial Intelligence (AI) research in Engineering Design (ED).

The study adopts a collaborative, expert-driven approach developed within the **Artificial Intelligence X Design (AIXDesign) Special Interest Group (SIG)** to identify and critically examine assumptions that shape how AI is conceptualized and investigated within the Engineering Design research community.

## Research Areas

The literature search covers five research areas:

1. **Human–AI Collaboration in Design**
2. **AI and Design Theory**
3. **AI and Early Stages of Design**
4. **AI and Detailed Design**
5. **AI Education in Design**

These areas represent different perspectives on the integration of AI into engineering design activities, theoretical frameworks, collaborative practices, and education.

## Repository Contents

The repository provides the materials used to identify and select the literature for analysis:

- **Scopus search queries:** The complete search strings used to retrieve publications for each research area.
- **Relevant papers:** The lists of the most-cited papers selected for further analysis in each research area.

The search queries combine three components: the ISSNs of relevant design journals and conference proceedings, AI-related keywords, and domain-specific keywords tailored to each research area.

## Literature Search and Paper Selection

The literature search was conducted using Scopus. The searches returned **565 papers** across the five research areas:

| Research Area | Papers Retrieved |
|---|---:|
| Human–AI Collaboration in Design | 51 |
| AI and Design Theory | 70 |
| AI and Early Stages of Design | 224 |
| AI and Detailed Design | 207 |
| AI Education in Design | 13 |
| **Total** | **565** |

The 20 most-cited papers published between 2016 and 2025 were selected for further analysis in each research area. Since the search for AI Education in Design returned only 13 papers, all retrieved papers in that area were included.

## Analytical Approach

The selected papers were examined by experts within the AIXDesign SIG. The analysis was informed by the problematisation approach proposed by Alvesson and Sandberg (2011), which encourages researchers to critically examine assumptions underlying existing research.

Following the initial analysis, the experts discussed, refined, and consolidated the identified assumptions. The selected papers were then revisited to identify supporting evidence and assess whether the assumptions were grounded in the literature.

## Purpose

This repository supports transparency by making the search strategy and selected literature available to other researchers. It provides a reference for understanding the materials used in the study and facilitates further investigation of implicit assumptions in AI-related Engineering Design research.
